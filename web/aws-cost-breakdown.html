AWS Cost Analysis: Compression vs No Compression

t3.xlarge instances ($119.81/month) - Showing compute, bandwidth, and total costs
Assumptions:
  • Compression reduces bandwidth by 70%
  • Compression adds 30% CPU/RAM overhead requiring more instances
  • ~10 TB bandwidth per instance before scaling needed
  • With compression overhead, ~7.7 TB bandwidth per instance
Break-Even Analysis: At approximately 5 TB/month bandwidth, compression starts becoming beneficial.(with free tier) The additional compute cost from compression overhead is offset by bandwidth savings.
